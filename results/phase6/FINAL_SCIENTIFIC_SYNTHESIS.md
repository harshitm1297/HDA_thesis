# Final data-only scientific synthesis through Phase 6

The project begins with one supplied proteomics workbook containing 84 specimens from 42 patients, with one tumour and one matched non-tumour specimen per patient. The analysis preserves that pairing at every inferential and predictive stage. It also preserves the original 8,071 feature rows under stable keys because supplied gene-style labels are not sufficient to reconstruct protein accessions, isoforms or protein groups. The result is an internally auditable reanalysis of the available matrix, not a reconstruction of the unprovided mass-spectrometry workflow.

Phase 1 found enough missingness and sample heterogeneity to require two parallel representations of the evidence: quantitative abundance among observed paired values and detection-pattern differences. Twelve samples, corresponding to seven patient pairs, met the prespecified multi-family QC flag rule. Those pairs were retained in the primary analysis and removed only as whole pairs in sensitivity analyses. Label-blind PCA used 3,759 eligible variable features and was treated as a diagnostic view, not as proof of biological subtypes or a preprocessing truth.

Under the Phase 3 frozen no-imputation primary analysis, 614 rows meet the abundance-candidate rule and 1,827 meet the separate detection-pattern rule. The two classes are deliberately not merged: an abundance contrast conditions on quantitative observations, whereas a detection contrast describes asymmetric observation patterns whose biological and technical causes cannot be separated with this matrix. Phase 4 maps only unambiguous measured labels to frozen Reactome release 86. It identifies 271 pathways supported by both ranked enrichment and patient-level paired scoring, but it does not accept any stable patient cluster and therefore makes no subtype claim.

Phase 5 demonstrates a strong internal tumour-versus-matched-non-tumour signal under patient-grouped nested validation. The leading abundance elastic-net model has median AUC 0.9824 across 25 repeated partitions, and its exact Phase 6 leave-one-patient-out refit has AUC 0.9847. A fold-local PCA baseline is close at median AUC 0.9694, the fitted elastic nets are dense rather than panel-like, and all specimens come from the same 42 paired patients. Accordingly, the predictive result is evidence of internal tissue-state separability, not screening performance, diagnosis, cancer specificity or deployment readiness. Nested resampling is essential in this high-dimensional setting because preprocessing, selection and tuning must occur without outer-test information ([Varma and Simon, 2006](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-7-91)).

Phase 6 shows that much of the signal survives reasonable analytical perturbation but also quantifies where conclusions depend on those choices. Of the Phase 3 tiered findings, 583 of 614 abundance candidates and 1,804 of 1,827 detection candidates retain their respective core rule in at least 80% of exact patient deletions. Of 783 Reactome pathways, 596 meet the joint direction and leading-edge stability criteria across 24 scenarios. The paired-label ML null and random-overlap null both place the observed structure beyond their generated null ranges. Nevertheless, the multiverse also changes core feature counts substantially, especially under median centring and relaxed completeness, showing why a single result list would overstate certainty.

The final frozen integration produces 165 high-priority internal candidates. This tier requires unambiguous identity, robustness in the relevant inferential branch, and an additional pathway or predictive stability dimension. It does not imply that all evidence types are independent: Phase 3, Phase 4 and Phase 5 reuse the same patients and measurements. The tier is therefore a disciplined follow-up queue, not a biomarker panel. The complete 8,071-row table retains effects, uncertainty, detection evidence, multiverse support, patient influence, pathway membership, ML stability, limitations and the exact permitted claim.

The work remains bounded by facts unavailable in the dataset: raw files, acquisition and batch information, peptide/protein-inference evidence, missingness causes, detailed anatomy and pathology, HPV and other clinical variables, the condition of matched non-tumour tissue, healthy and disease controls, outcomes, an independent cohort and orthogonal assays. Research can justify safeguards but cannot impute these project-specific facts. Phase 2 also remains unimplemented, so sensitivity to alternative missing-value preprocessing has not been benchmarked. The appropriate next scientific step inside this repository is Phase 2, followed by regeneration of Phases 3–6; the appropriate biological next step would require new data and therefore lies outside the present data-only scope.

The defensible project-level conclusion is narrow and useful: this paired oral-cancer tissue proteomics dataset contains reproducible internal structure across feature inference, pathways and leakage-controlled prediction, and it yields a transparent set of candidates for future validation. It cannot establish a deployable biomarker, clinical utility, causal biology, population generalisability or disease specificity. The claim ledger and limitation register are part of the result, not after-the-fact disclaimers.
